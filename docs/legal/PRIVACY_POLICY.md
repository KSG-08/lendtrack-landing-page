# LendTrack — Privacy Policy

**Effective 1 August 2026**

## Overview

LendTrack is a personal lending tracker that helps you record items you have
lent to other people and when they are due back. This policy explains what
information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

- **Account information:** when you sign up with email and password, or with
  Google Sign-In, we receive your email address, display name, and (for Google
  accounts) your profile photo.
- **Lending records you create:** the item name, the borrower's name and
  optional phone number, category, dates, notes, and any photo you attach.
  Photos are stored inside your lending record.
- **Diagnostic data:** if the app crashes, anonymous crash reports are sent to
  Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix problems. These do not include the
  contents of your lending records.

## How your information is used

Your data is used solely to provide the app's features: storing and displaying
your lendings, signing you in, and sending you local reminders about due dates.
We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Where your data is stored

Your account and lending records are stored using Google Firebase
(Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Data is protected by security rules so
that only your signed-in account can read or write your records. See Google's
privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy for how Google processes
data on our behalf.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** used only when you choose to attach a photo to a lending.
- **Notifications:** used to remind you about upcoming and overdue due dates.
  You can disable reminders in the app or in your device settings.

## Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated lending data at any
time from **Profile → Delete Account**. Deletion is immediate and cannot be
undone.

## Children

LendTrack is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@lend-track.com.
