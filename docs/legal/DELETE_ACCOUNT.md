# LendTrack — Delete Your Account

**Effective 1 August 2026**

> Hosted at /legal/delete-account/. This is the URL entered in the Play Console
> under App content → Data deletion. It must stay publicly reachable without
> signing in, including for people who have already uninstalled the app.

## Overview

This page explains how to permanently delete your LendTrack account, published
by the LendTrack team, along with every piece of data the app holds about you.
There are two ways to do it — one inside the app, and one by email if you no
longer have the app installed.

## Option 1 — Delete it yourself in the app

Deletion is immediate and needs no approval from us.

1. Open LendTrack and sign in to the account you want to delete.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab.
3. Tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm it is you — enter your password, or re-confirm with Google if you
   signed up with Google Sign-In.
5. Tap **Delete my account**, then confirm once more.

Your account and records are erased at that moment and you are signed out. This
cannot be undone, so note down anything you want to keep before you start.

## Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you have already uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in, email
support@lend-track.com from the email address on the account, with the subject
**Delete my account**.

We verify the request came from the account owner, then delete the account and
all of its data. Requests are actioned **within 30 days** of receipt, and we
reply to confirm once it is done.

## What gets deleted

- **Your sign-in record** — email address, password credential, or the link to
  your Google account.
- **Your profile** — display name, email address, and profile photo where one
  was supplied by Google Sign-In.
- **Every lending record you have created** — item names, borrower names and
  phone numbers, categories, dates, notes, return details, and any photos you
  attached. Photos are stored inside the lending record itself, so they are
  removed with it; there is no separate copy left behind.
- **Everything stored on your device** — records queued while you were offline,
  and cached photos.

## What is kept, and for how long

- **Your account, profile, and lending records** are not kept. They are deleted
  immediately and cannot be recovered.
- **Anonymous crash diagnostics** (Firebase Crashlytics) are retained until they
  automatically expire, within 90 days. These reports are anonymous and never
  contain the contents of your lending records.

Deleted data may persist briefly in our hosting provider's routine backups
before those backups age out. It is not accessible through the app and is not
used for any purpose.
